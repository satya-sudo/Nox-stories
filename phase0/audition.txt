Good evening. I'm Nox. Tonight, I have a story about a forgotten library, a very small dragon, and a promise that took a hundred years to keep.

At midnight, someone knocked on the library door. Mara looked up from her tea. Nobody had visited since the first snow, and the road outside had disappeared beneath a silver blanket. Three quiet knocks came again. Then a voice said, "I believe this book is overdue."

On the doorstep stood a dragon no larger than a house cat. Rain glittered on his blue scales. Beneath one wing, he carried a book tied with a faded green ribbon. Mara recognized the ribbon immediately. Her grandmother had used it to mark the stories she loved most.

"How overdue?" Mara asked. The dragon lowered his head. "That depends," he whispered, "on whether you count the years I spent asleep." From somewhere behind the shelves, a clock began to strike. It had not worked in thirty years.

Mara stepped aside. "Come in, then. We'll need another cup." The dragon smiled, and the smallest flame warmed the kettle. For a moment, the library smelled of cinnamon and distant summer rain.

Outside, the snow kept falling. Inside, two strangers opened a book. Whatever waited in its pages, they would face it together. And sometimes, my friend, that is where the best adventures begin.
